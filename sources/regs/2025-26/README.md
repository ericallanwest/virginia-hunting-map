# 2025–26 regulations (archive source)

DWR removed the 2025–26 *Hunting and Trapping in Virginia Regulations Digest* PDF from its sites
(`dwr.virginia.gov` and `dwr.am.virginia.gov` both return 404 for it as of Nov 2026). The same guide
is still published as web pages by eRegulations (DWR's guide publisher). These are snapshots of those
pages, saved 2026-11-11, from `https://www.eregulations.com/virginia/hunting/<page>`.

Most pages say "Last Updated: March 30–April 1, 2026" and show 2025–26 dates (e.g. bear/turkey archery
Oct 4 – Nov 14, urban archery Sep 6 – Oct 3, NOVA late archery Mar 30 – Apr 26, 2026). `game-bird.html`
was updated Nov 3, 2026 and is migratory-bird content, not used by the app.

They are the source for encoding the 2025–26 license year (`src/regs/*-2025.ts`), if/when added.

The official 2025–26 digest PDF (31 MB, sha256 `19e03a91…5c7075`, from eRegulations — no longer on dwr.virginia.gov) and the 2024–25 digest PDF (48 MB, sha256 `51741445…d7bdda`) are archived as attachments to the GitHub release
[source-digests](https://github.com/ericallanwest/virginia-hunting-map/releases/tag/source-digests).
